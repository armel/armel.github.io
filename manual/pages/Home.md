# About this firmware

This firmware is a fork of the [F4HWN custom firmware](https://github.com/armel/uv-k5-firmware-custom). It extends the work originally done for the UV-K5 V1 (DP32G030 MCU) and adapts it to the newer UV-K1 and UV-K5 V3, which are based on the PY32F071 MCU. It is the result of joint work by [@muzkr](https://github.com/muzkr) and [@armel](https://github.com/armel). Many thanks also to [@mrkusypl](https://github.com/mrkusypl) for his valuable help, as well as to everyone who has contributed to the project from time to time.

Starting with `v6.0.0`, the firmware is distributed in four official editions that share the same core but target different uses. `Fusion` is the recommended balanced edition for everyday use; `FieldOps` adds RescueOps, FoxHunt, and Beacon; `Transfer` adds AirCopy and Beam; and `Labs` adds the experimental overlay-app platform.

If you are new to the firmware, a good reading order is: [Getting started](./Getting-started), [Programming with CHIRP](./Programming-with-CHIRP) if you manage the radio from a computer, [Radio operation](./Radio-operation), [Scanning](./Scanning), [Menu](./Menu), then [Button functions](./Button-functions). If you prefer visual walkthroughs, see [Videos and tutorials](./Videos-and-tutorials).

If you are upgrading from an earlier build, see [Recent changes](./Recent-changes) for the latest stable `v6.1.0` changes and the earlier `v6.0.0` highlights.

> [!WARNING]
> THIS FIRMWARE HAS NO REAL BRAIN. PLEASE USE YOUR OWN.
> Use this firmware entirely at your own risk. There is absolutely no guarantee that it will work in any way, shape, or form on your radio(s). It may even brick your radio(s), in which case you would need to buy another radio.
>
> You have been warned.
> That said, have fun.
