# FoxHunt and Beacon

This compatibility page preserves links created for firmware versions where `FOX HUNT / BEACON` was one combined action.

Since `v6.0.0`, they are two independent applications and two independent programmable actions:

* [FoxHunt](./Fox-Hunt) is a receive-only direction-finding tool.
* [Beacon](./Beacon) is an automatic Morse transmitter.

The combined name is retained only when describing the historical `v5.8.0` and `v5.9.0` implementation. Current instructions, controls, availability, and safety information are maintained on the two separate pages above.
