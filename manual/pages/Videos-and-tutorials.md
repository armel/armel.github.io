# Videos and tutorials

This page points to the `F4HWN` YouTube channel as a companion to the written wiki.

The wiki remains the best reference for menus, exact wording, and step-by-step checks. The videos are useful when you want to see the radio, the screen, and the workflow in real conditions.

## F4HWN YouTube channel

The main channel is:

* [F4HWN on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@f4hwn)

It contains many videos about Quansheng radios, the custom firmware family, and related tools and workflows.

The channel also includes a dedicated `Quansheng` playlist, which is a convenient way to browse the most relevant videos in one place:

* [Quansheng playlist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MxBRIqZQaI8&list=PLpvxJU6jpRwKCzghVAzuIW2XUXSKZt4mR)

## Selected videos

Here are a few hand-picked videos from the channel:

* [Aviation Frequencies and MONITOR ✈️: Channel ≠ Frequency (the mistake that changes everything)!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dpf3QzkDdaQ): do you only hear some aeronautical frequencies, in AM with an 8.33 kHz step, when you open MONITOR? In many cases, this is neither a sensitivity issue nor a firmware issue !
* [New F4HWN Firmware v5.3.0 Fusion Edition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zEzYc3dlQNI): overview of the `v5.3.0` Fusion release, with the main changes and a practical introduction to what is new in that version.
* [S-Meter Calibration on F4HWN Fusion Edition with a TinySA Ultra](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QTlp5G5n_2Y): explains how to refine the S-meter calibration with a `TinySA Ultra`, then apply a correction in `CHIRP` if needed.
* [Is Your UV-K5 V1 Bricked? Here Is How to Repair It](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4cWtYH_bpro): recovery tutorial for a `UV-K5 V1` accidentally bricked after flashing the wrong firmware, using `ST-LINK`, `OpenOCD`, and bootloader restoration.

## Language

Many videos are in French.

If you are not a French speaker:

* enable subtitles when available
* use YouTube auto-translation if needed
* some videos may also provide English dubbing

## What the videos are good for

The videos are especially useful for:

* first-time orientation
* seeing menu navigation and button sequences
* programming with `CHIRP`
* understanding scan behavior
* exploring features such as UV Studio, [AirCopy](./AirCopy), or the spectrum analyzer
