Why

Follow the users

Labs became the edition most people run, for its apps. It deserves the main name.

A lighter daily firmware

AirCopy and raw flash writes leave the everyday build. That frees room for the app interface and keeps risky flash commands out of daily use.

Tools in one place

Cloning, Beam, flash backup and factory restore all move radio data around. They belong together in Service.

Plan for v7.0, details may still change. Custom builds keep every option open.
